## Research Interests

Public health and data science, with a focus on child maltreatment prevention, maternal and child health, and oral health. I integrate multi-sector administrative and clinical data with statistical modeling and interpretable machine learning to support early risk detection and evidence-based policy. I also work on infectious disease epidemiology, including nonlinear time-series and empirical dynamic modeling.

**Keywords:** child maltreatment · dental neglect · maternal and child health · infectious disease epidemiology · machine learning · time-series analysis · empirical dynamic modeling (EDM)
